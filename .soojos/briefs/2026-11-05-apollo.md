# Brief: Apollo — spoken SKLPC Gujarati app

Approved 2026-11-05 (revised after user correction: no family recordings available).

- **Intent:** Build Apollo so Sayuj gains confidence holding everyday spoken conversations in the SKLPC (Kutch Leva Patel) variety of Gujarati. Listening and speaking only; no script.
- **Deeper version:** Every dialect item is marked with how it differs from standard Gujarati and how sure we are, so standard Gujarati never passes as dialect.
- **Raw material:** Public SKLPC material worldwide (samaj sites, gaam pages, Facebook/YouTube/Instagram video and captions), Kutch dialect linguistics (Kutchi vs. Kutchi-Gujarati), standard spoken-Gujarati references as fallback.
- **Methods and tools:** Parallel source sweep → `knowledge/reports/apollo-sklpc-dialect.md` + claims in `claims.jsonl` (URL + date each). Phone-first static PWA in `projects/apollo/app/`: lessons, flashcards with spaced repetition, role-play scenes, Gujarati TTS for listening, speech practice.
- **Outside check:** (1) Automated: `node projects/apollo/test/check.mjs`. It validates every phrase has source+date+confidence, then drives the app in headless Chromium through a lesson, a review and a scene with zero console errors. (2) Human, no recording needed: the in-app "Ask family" mode lets Sayuj mark each dialect phrase ✓/✗ after saying it to a parent; exported results are the real verdict. Until that's done, dialect content is **unverified**.
