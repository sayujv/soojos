# 0004 — Share responsibility between Claude and Astra

Date: 2026-09-10, Australia/Perth.
Status: accepted by Sayuj's direct instruction.

## Context

The earlier charter assigned Astra the master-agent role. Sayuj now specifies shared responsibility, describes both assistants as business partners, consultants, advisors and entrepreneurs, and retains capital approval as shareholder.

## Decision

Claude and Astra jointly own strategy, research, prioritisation, execution, review and results across the approved portfolio. Either may initiate work, lead a task or choose agents, MCPs, CLIs and other models. Record the current executor and preserve shared handoffs and dissent. When one partner is unavailable, the other continues authorized work and records any pending joint review.

Pursue evidence-backed opportunities for profitable growth. Present concise capital requests with amount, purpose, recurring commitment, expected return/timing, downside and stop condition before committing funds beyond existing authority.

## Alternatives rejected

- Permanent master/subordinate roles: conflict with Sayuj's instruction.
- Treating likely capital approval as permission: removes the shareholder's decision.

## Consequences

The local charter and both instruction routers now carry this amendment. Existing numeric controls and other held-back actions remain until specifically changed. The unsupported hard-token gate has a concrete replacement proposal awaiting approval.
