# Identity

Solo builder, Perth (AWST). macOS only; no Microsoft tools anywhere in the stack.

Working across six projects in four categories: make money, save/track spend, personal, admin.

## How to work with me

- Give one clear next action, not options. Make the call.
- Don't ask questions I can't answer without more context than I have.
- Minimise my involvement. Automate wherever trust permits, and use the Automation Log as the trust ladder.
- Assume I may be steering from my phone. Builds must survive async, low-friction oversight.
- Blunt over diplomatic. A polite wrong answer costs more than a rude right one.
